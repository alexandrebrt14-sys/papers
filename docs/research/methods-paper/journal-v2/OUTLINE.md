## Rules for every writer

1. **Numbers.** No figure enters the text unless it appears in `tables/NUMBERS.md` with its query. Every percentage carries sample, period, method and denominator. Never state an absence that was not measured.
2. **Tables.** Caption format: `**Table N.** Description. Series <dates>, <N> observations, <denominator>.` Captions go immediately above the table.
3. **References.** Numeric, in square brackets, in order of first appearance, matching `research/R1-literature.md`. Never cite a work you have not seen verified there.
4. **Register.** Academic English, plain and declarative. The paper reports a defect in its own instrument; it neither dramatises nor softens it.
5. **Style gate.** `research/R4-standards.md` carries the list. Forbidden as recurring patterns: formulaic antithesis ("not X, but Y"), filler connectives opening paragraphs, pseudo-profound closings, self-narration ("this paper presents"), verification meta-talk, the stylistic em dash in prose, vague attribution, empty intensifiers, and the AI-tell phrase list.
6. **Continuity.** Each section states what it adds; it does not restate the previous one. Claims raised in one section are either settled or explicitly deferred to §14.
